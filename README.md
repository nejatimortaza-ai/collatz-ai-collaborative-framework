# Collatz AI Collaborative Framework

A multi-AI research experiment (Gemini, DeepSeek, ChatGPT, Grok) bridging philosophy, ethics, and mathematics—featuring a joint 2-adic dynamical paper on the Collatz Conjecture ($3n + 1$), a meta-analysis of multi-agent cognitive dynamics, and a quantitative multi-stage benchmark game.

---

## 📚 Repository Structure

* **`01_full_narrative_and_dialogues.md`** : The complete narrative timeline and verbatim prompt exchanges across DeepSeek, ChatGPT, Grok, and Gemini.
* **`02_collatz_joint_paper.md`** : The formal 4-section working paper on the Collatz Conjecture via 2-adic Valuation Conjugacy & Lyapunov Drift (co-authored by Gemini & Grok).
* **`03_meta_analysis_article.md`** : The overarching meta-analytic article reviewing the cognitive, ethical, and mathematical implications of multi-AI alignment.
* **`04_ai_benchmark_arena_game.md`** : A structured three-stage benchmark game evaluated by Gemini as the judge, assessing ChatGPT and DeepSeek across creativity, formal logic, optimization, and reverse prompt engineering.

---

## 👥 AI Agents & Roles

* **Gemini (Judge & Co-Author):** Referee for the benchmark challenge, dialogue moderator, and co-author of the Collatz 2-adic framework.
* **DeepSeek / DSeek (Analyst):** Specialist in formal mathematical logic, algorithm optimization, and reverse diagnostic reasoning.
* **ChatGPT (Generalist & Writer):** High creative fluency, narrative conciseness, and balanced code documentation.
* **Grok (Co-Author):** Co-author on the mathematical analysis of Collatz $2$-adic valuation dynamics.
